Aurasity

Untangle Your Day

No two minds work the same way. Aurasity starts with a short, five-question test to understand how you work, then adjusts your focus timers, routines, and interface to fit you. There are no right or wrong answers — the result isn't a label, it's a starting point.

Focus

The working system that fits you shows up ready: short micro sessions, classic pomodoro, or long deep-work blocks. You get a warning before a session ends, and the timer keeps running correctly even while your phone is locked.

Tasks

Write down whatever's on your mind in one line, organize it later. Break a task that feels overwhelming into small, doable steps. If you can't finish something today, push it to tomorrow instead of deleting it.

Routine

Your morning and evening routines arrive already shaped to your profile. Check off your habits and watch your streak build. Breaking a streak never triggers a blaming message — missing a day doesn't erase what you've built.

Aura

A chat companion you can talk to about how your day is going — it offers advice and tries to lift your mood. It listens, suggests one small step, and only adds that step to the app with your approval.

Profile

Your profile from the five-question test, your stats, and every app setting live in one place. Revisit it whenever you like.

Aurasity Pro

Focus, Tasks, Routine, and Profile are completely free. Aura unlocks with an Aurasity Pro subscription: $4.99 per month or $39.99 per year (price may vary by region). There is no free trial; the subscription renews automatically unless you cancel. Cancel anytime in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions on your iPhone.

Aurasity is not a medical app and does not provide a diagnosis. The profile test and Aura are tools for self-awareness and everyday support; they do not substitute for a medical evaluation. If you're in an emergency, call your local emergency number.
