1. Description of the service

Aurasity is a productivity app that offers its focus (timer), tasks, routine planning, and profile/statistics sections free of charge. It also includes Aura, an optional AI chat companion; Aura unlocks with an Aurasity Pro subscription (see Section 4).

2. The nature of the app — not a medical device

Aurasity is not a medical device, does not provide a diagnosis, and does not offer treatment. The profile test is a structured tool that helps you recognize your own working tendencies; its results cannot be interpreted as a medical evaluation.

Aura is not a source of medical or psychological advice. Aura is a chat companion that listens to your everyday concerns, tries to lift your mood, and offers small suggestions — it is not a therapist, a doctor, or a crisis line, and it does not act as one.

If you're in an emergency, call your local emergency number. If you have a serious concern about your mental health, consult a professional. When Aura detects a message carrying a self-harm signal, it stops the conversation and shows a fixed card directing you to a professional; that is the most Aura is able to do.

3. AI can make mistakes

Aura's replies are generated by an AI model (Google Gemini). These replies may be incorrect, incomplete, out of date, or misleading. Don't blindly trust anything Aura says; you are responsible for the decisions you make, especially on matters such as health, legal, or financial topics.

Aura never modifies any of your records on its own: when it suggests adding, deleting, or postponing a task/routine item, it first asks for your approval, and the app itself only performs the action once you approve it.

5. Fair use — 100 messages per week

While using Aura, a fair-use limit of 100 messages per week per person applies. The counter resets every Monday.

This limit exists so everyone can access Aura fairly and to prevent abuse of the service. On the Aura Pro screen you'll see the phrase "fair use" instead of this number; the word "unlimited" never appears anywhere, because that would be misleading given that a limit exists behind the scenes. As you approach the limit (around 80% of it) you'll see a gentle reminder; once you reach it, Aura will tell you it renews on Monday.
